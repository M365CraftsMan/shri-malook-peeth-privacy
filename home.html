श्री मलूक पीठ - श्रीधाम वृन्दावन

App Purpose: This application is designed for devotees to track their daily "Naam Jap" (Chanting) progress. It provides an offline counter, a global leaderboard to encourage spiritual consistency, and access to sacred texts (Aarti) and daily Darshan from Shri Malook Peeth, Vrindavan.

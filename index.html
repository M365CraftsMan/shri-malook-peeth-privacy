# Privacy Policy for Shri Malook Peeth App **Last Updated:** August 2, 2026 ## 1. Introduction Welcome to the **Shri Malook Peeth** mobile application (the **"App"**). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how we protect it, and the choices available to you when using the App. It applies to all features of the App, including Google Sign-In, the Naam Jap Counter, Cloud Synchronization, and the Global Leaderboard. By using this App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. --- ## 2. Information We Collect We collect certain information to provide and improve the services offered through the App. ### Personal Information When you sign in using your Google Account, we may receive: * Your name * Email address * Profile photo * A unique Google account identifier, as permitted by your Google account settings ### Sadhana Data To help you track your spiritual practice (Sadhana), we collect: * Naam Jap counts * Completed Mala counts * Daily progress * Lifetime Mala count * Activity timestamps ### Device Information We may collect limited technical information, including: * Device model * Android version * Device identifiers required for Firebase services * Crash reports and diagnostic information * Notification tokens (where applicable) This information is used solely to improve the reliability, security, and functionality of the App. --- ## 3. How We Use Your Information We use the information we collect for the following purposes: ### Account Management To create your account, personalize your experience, and securely authenticate you using Google Sign-In. ### Cloud Backup and Synchronization To securely synchronize your spiritual progress across devices and restore your Lifetime Mala Count when you sign in on another device. ### Global Leaderboard To calculate and display your ranking among other devotees who choose to participate in the Global Leaderboard. ### Reminders and Notifications To schedule local reminders and, where applicable, send push notifications related to your Naam Jap practice. ### App Improvement To monitor app performance, identify crashes, fix bugs, and improve the overall user experience. --- ## 4. Global Leaderboard and Public Information ### Important Notice Participation in the **Global Leaderboard** is voluntary. By enabling **Cloud Sync**, you choose to participate in the Global Leaderboard. The following information will become visible to all users of the App: * Your display name (provided by Google) * Your profile photo (provided by Google) * Your Lifetime Mala Count **Your email address, Google account identifier, and any other personal information are never displayed publicly or shared with other users through the App.** --- ## 5. Data Storage and Security ### Local Storage Your daily progress is stored locally on your device using the Room database. ### Cloud Storage If Cloud Sync is enabled, your data is securely stored using Google Firebase Firestore. Data transmitted between the App and Firebase is protected using Google's security infrastructure and industry-standard encryption during transmission and storage. ### Data Retention We retain your cloud data while your account remains active or until you request deletion, unless a longer retention period is required by applicable law. --- ## 6. Third-Party Services The App uses trusted third-party services, including: * Google Sign-In (Authentication) * Google Firebase (Cloud database, analytics, crash reporting, and notifications) * YouTube (Embedded devotional video content) These services maintain their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to review them before using the App. --- ## 7. App Permissions The App may request the following permissions when necessary: * **Internet Access** – Required for Google Sign-In, Firebase synchronization, and loading online content. * **Notifications** – Required to deliver Naam Jap reminders and other app notifications. The App requests only the permissions necessary to provide its intended functionality. --- ## 8. Your Rights Subject to applicable laws, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India), you may have the following rights: * **Right to Access** – View the information associated with your account. * **Right to Correction** – Update your Google Account information, which will be reflected within the App. * **Right to Erasure** – Request deletion of your account and associated cloud data. * **Right to Withdraw Consent** – Disable Cloud Sync or discontinue use of the App at any time. To exercise these rights, please contact us using the details provided below. --- ## 9. Children's Privacy The App is intended for general audiences and is not specifically directed toward children.
